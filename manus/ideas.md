<text>
<idea>
  <design_movement>Academic Brutalism meets Cybernetic Chaos</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Raw Data Aesthetic**: Expose the underlying structures (grids, monospaced fonts, visible borders) to reflect the "skeleton" of the theory.
    2. **Chaos vs. Order**: Juxtapose strict mathematical layouts with chaotic, generative visual elements (glitch effects, noise).
    3. **Interactive Exploration**: Users shouldn't just read; they should "manipulate" the parameters to see the chaos emerge.
    4. **Information Density**: High-density layouts that resemble scientific dashboards or lab monitors.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **"The Band Merging Point"**:
    - **Primary**: Deep Void Black (#0a0a0a) - The canvas of the unknown.
    - **Accent**: Electric Blue (#3b82f6) - Representing the orderly prime distribution.
    - **Critical**: Chaos Red (#ef4444) - Highlighting the chaotic regions and band merging points.
    - **Text**: Off-white/Silver (#e5e7eb) - For readability against the dark void.
    *Reasoning*: High contrast to emphasize the precision of mathematics against the darkness of the unknown.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **"The Bifurcation Grid"**:
    - Asymmetric split-screen layouts: Theory on one side, live simulation on the other.
    - Sticky side navigation that resembles a code editor's file tree.
    - Content blocks separated by thick, visible borders (1px solid).
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Generative Backgrounds**: Subtle, slow-moving logistic map visualizations as section backgrounds.
    2. **Monospaced Headers**: Use a coding font (JetBrains Mono or Fira Code) for all headings to emphasize the computational nature.
    3. **"Glitch" Hover Effects**: Interactive elements slightly distort or shift RGB channels on hover, hinting at the underlying chaos.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **"Parameter Control"**:
    - Sliders and inputs are not just UI; they are the primary way to navigate the content.
    - Scroll-triggered animations that "evolve" the equations or graphs as the user reads.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Staggered Reveal**: Content loads in chunks, like data being received from a satellite.
    - **Mathematical Transitions**: Graphs don't just fade in; they "grow" or "iterate" into existence.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Headings**: `JetBrains Mono` (or similar monospace) - Bold, uppercase, tracking-tight.
    - **Body**: `Inter` or `Switzer` - Clean sans-serif for high readability of dense text.
    - **Math**: `KaTeX` style serif for equations.
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  <design_movement>Neo-Swiss / International Typographic Style</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Mathematical Purity**: Absolute reliance on grid systems and whitespace to convey order.
    2. **Typography as Image**: Large, bold typography replaces decorative images.
    3. **Clarity above all**: The complex theory must be broken down into digestible, clean modules.
    4. **Subtle Complexity**: Complexity is hidden in the details (micro-interactions), not the layout.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **"The Prime Spectrum"**:
    - **Background**: Stark White (#ffffff) or Very Light Grey (#f8f9fa).
    - **Foreground**: Swiss Red (#ff3b30) and Deep Blue (#007aff).
    - **Text**: Ink Black (#1c1c1e).
    *Reasoning*: Classic, authoritative, and clean. Allows the data visualizations to pop without distraction.
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **"The Modular Grid"**:
    - Strict 12-column grid.
    - Content is compartmentalized into "cards" or "blocks" with generous padding.
    - Asymmetric balance: Large text on the left, detailed charts on the right.
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Oversized Typography**: Section numbers (01, 02, 03) are massive and structural.
    2. **Thin Lines**: Hairline dividers to separate content.
    3. **Abstract Geometric Shapes**: Simple circles and squares to represent prime/composite states.
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **"Rational Interaction"**:
    - Hover states are crisp and immediate (no soft fades).
    - Tooltips provide deep dives into terms without cluttering the main text.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Slide and Snap**: Elements slide into place with precision.
    - **Parallax**: Subtle depth between text and background elements.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Primary**: `Helvetica Now` or `Unica77` (Neo-grotesque).
    - **Hierarchy**: Extreme contrast in size between headings and body text.
  </typography_system>
</idea>
</text>

<idea>
  <design_movement>Ethereal Sci-Fi / Glassmorphism</design_movement>
  <core_principles>
    1. **Translucency & Depth**: Using blur and glass effects to layer information.
    2. **Organic Flow**: Smooth curves and gradients to represent the "natural" distribution of primes.
    3. **Immersive Atmosphere**: Dark mode by default, glowing elements.
    4. **Data as Art**: Visualizations are treated as beautiful objects, not just charts.
  </core_principles>
  <color_philosophy>
    **"Nebula of Numbers"**:
    - **Background**: Deep Indigo/Purple gradients (#1e1b4b to #312e81).
    - **Glass**: White with low opacity and blur.
    - **Glow**: Cyan (#22d3ee) and Magenta (#d946ef).
    *Reasoning*: Evokes a sense of mystery and deep space exploration, fitting for "OpenSciEval".
  </color_philosophy>
  <layout_paradigm>
    **"The Floating Canvas"**:
    - Content cards "float" in 3D space.
    - Z-axis layering: Background (stars/particles), Middle (glass cards), Foreground (text/data).
  </layout_paradigm>
  <signature_elements>
    1. **Glass Cards**: Frosted glass effect for all containers.
    2. **Glowing Borders**: Subtle gradients on borders to define edges.
    3. **Particle Effects**: Background particles that react to mouse movement (representing primes).
  </signature_elements>
  <interaction_philosophy>
    **"Fluid Discovery"**:
    - Smooth scrolling with inertia.
    - Elements "drift" slightly.
  </interaction_philosophy>
  <animation>
    - **Blur-in**: Content blurs into focus.
    - **Float**: Idle animations for cards.
  </animation>
  <typography_system>
    - **Headings**: `Sora` or `Outfit` (Modern geometric sans).
    - **Body**: `Inter` or `DM Sans`.
  </typography_system>
</idea>
